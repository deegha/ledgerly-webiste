## Where to find it

**Settings → Fiscal years.** An **Owner** creates fiscal years; period-level actions
(soft-close, hard-close, reopen) happen on **Accounting → Periods**.

![Settings → Fiscal years: a list of fiscal years (FY2027-28 running Apr 2027 – Mar 2028, FY2026-27 running Apr 2026 – Mar 2027, each with 12 periods) above a "Create a new fiscal year" form with a start date and a label](/guide/settings/fiscal-years.jpg "The fiscal years your books hold, and the form to add the next one")

## What's here

- A **list** of every fiscal year your books hold, with its date range and period count.
  Ledgerly can hold several at once.
- A **Create a new fiscal year** form: pick a **start date** and give it a **label**
  (e.g. `FY2027-28`). It runs **12 calendar months** from that date and is split into
  **12 monthly periods** automatically.

## When to add one

Add the next fiscal year **before it starts**, so there's always an open period to post into.
If you issue an invoice dated in a period that doesn't exist yet, it can't post.

The concepts behind fiscal years and why Sri Lanka's runs April–March are covered in
[What a fiscal year is](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/fiscal-years).
