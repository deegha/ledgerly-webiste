## What this is

The organization profile is your business's identity in Ledgerly. Most of it is set during
[onboarding](/guide/getting-started/onboarding); this screen is where you keep it current.

## Where to find it

**Settings → Organization.** You need to be an **Owner** to change anything here.

![Settings → Organization: trading name, legal name, TIN, VAT registration number, business registration number, a VAT-registered toggle, contact fields, address fields, and a logo upload area](/guide/settings/organization-profile.jpg "The organization profile — identity, tax registration, address, and logo")

## The fields

- **Trading name** — the name you operate under and see throughout the app.
- **Legal name** — the registered entity name; this is what prints on documents.
- **TIN** — your Taxpayer Identification Number.
- **VAT registration no.** and the **VAT registered** toggle — turning this on unlocks the VAT
  fields on invoices and the VAT Return. Only turn it on when you're actually registered.
- **Business registration no.** — your company or business registration number.
- **Email**, **Phone**, **Address** — used on your invoices and other issued documents. Enter
  the address as it should appear to a customer.
- **Logo** — drop or browse for an image (up to 2 MB). It's used on the
  [invoice template](/guide/settings/invoice-template).

## Common questions

**We changed our trading name.** Update it here — it takes effect across the app immediately.
Documents already issued keep the name they were issued under.

**We just registered for VAT.** Turn on the VAT toggle and enter the registration number. New
invoices will carry VAT fields from then on; nothing already posted changes.
