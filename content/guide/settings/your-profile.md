## Where to find it

**Settings → Profile.** This is _your_ account, not the organization's — it's the same
wherever you're a member.

![Settings → Profile: a photo with Change/Remove, First name and Last name fields, a disabled Email field noted as the sign-in identifier, a Save changes button, and a Password section with current, new and confirm fields](/guide/settings/your-profile.jpg "Your name and photo, and a separate section to change your password")

## What you can change

- **Photo** — a JPG or PNG up to 2 MB. It appears next to your name and on entries you post.
- **First and last name** — how you appear throughout the app and on the "posted by" stamp of
  everything you do. Changing it doesn't rewrite past entries' display; it updates going
  forward.
- **Password** — enter your **current** password, then a **new** one (at least 12 characters,
  mixed case, and a digit) twice.

## What you can't change here

Your **email is your sign-in identifier** and is fixed on this screen. If it genuinely needs
to change, that's an Owner/support task, not a self-service one.

## Common questions

**I changed my password — am I signed out elsewhere?** Check
[Settings → Sessions](/guide/settings/security-and-sessions) and revoke any other sessions
yourself if you want them ended.
