## Where to find it

**Settings → Invoice template.** An **Owner** or **Accountant** can edit it.

![Settings → Invoice template: a "Show TAX INVOICE label" toggle (on), a "Show amount in words" toggle (off), a footer-text box reading "This is a computer-generated invoice and does not require a signature.", a logo upload area, and a Bank details section](/guide/settings/invoice-template.jpg "The template — a compliant layout with your logo, footer, and payment details layered on")

## What you control

The **layout itself is gazette-compliant** (Gazette 2481/22) and you don't design it. What you
set here is the content that goes on top:

- **Show "TAX INVOICE" label** — the heading required on a tax invoice. On by default.
- **Show amount in words** — prints the total spelled out as well as in figures.
- **Footer text** — a line at the bottom of every invoice, e.g. "This is a computer-generated
  invoice and does not require a signature."
- **Logo** — shown at the top of every generated invoice PDF (separate from the app logo,
  though you'll usually use the same image).
- **Bank details** — bank name, account name, account number, branch. Printed as payment
  instructions on every invoice PDF. **Leave blank to omit the section** entirely.

## Before you rely on it

Preview an invoice PDF after any change. The compliance requirements around the invoice format
are covered in [the Compliance chapter](/guide/compliance).
