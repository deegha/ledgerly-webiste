## Where to find it

**Settings → Team.** An **Owner** invites, re-roles, and deactivates people here.

![Settings → Team: a members table with name, email and role, and an "Invite a member" form below it](/guide/settings/team-members.jpg "Settings → Team — current members and the invite form")

## The full walkthrough

Sending an invitation, choosing a role, changing someone's access, and deactivating a person
who has left — all of it is in **[Inviting your
team](/guide/getting-started/inviting-your-team)** in the Getting Started chapter.

The short version:

- **Invite** — enter an email (their login), optionally a name, pick a role, send. They get a
  link to set a password and join.
- **Change a role** — the dropdown on each member row; takes effect immediately, past work
  untouched.
- **Deactivate** — removes access without deleting anything they've posted.
- You can't change your own role or deactivate yourself — the guard against an ownerless
  organization.
