## What it is

A **fiscal year** (or financial year) is the 12-month span your accounts are drawn up for.
Your annual profit, your year-end Balance Sheet, and your tax return are all "for the fiscal
year". It's the top-level container; the [12 monthly
periods](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/accounting-periods) sit inside it.

![The Fiscal years settings screen: FY2027-28 running Apr 01, 2027 – Mar 31, 2028, FY2026-27 running Apr 01, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027, each with 12 periods, and a "Create a new fiscal year" form](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/fiscal-years.jpg "Each fiscal year runs 12 months from its start date and is split into monthly periods")

## Why it isn't January to December

A fiscal year just has to be 12 months long — it can start in any month. Sri Lanka's standard
is **1 April to 31 March**, so that's Ledgerly's default and what you'll see unless you chose
otherwise during [onboarding](/guide/getting-started/onboarding).

Businesses use a non-calendar year for practical reasons: aligning with the tax authority's
year, closing the books in a quiet trading month rather than during the December rush, or
matching a parent company. What matters is that it's **consistent** — this year's results are
only comparable to last year's if both cover the same 12 months.

## How Ledgerly uses it

- Creating a fiscal year generates its **12 monthly periods** automatically — one calendar
  month each, running from the start date.
- A journal entry's **date** places it in a period, and that period's fiscal year.
- The **Profit & Loss** and **Cash Flow** reports are "for a period" — usually a fiscal year
  or a range of its months. The **Balance Sheet** is "as at a date", typically the fiscal
  year end.
- At the end of a fiscal year you **close** it: the year's profit is rolled into Retained
  Earnings, and the year is sealed.

## Running more than one

Your books can hold several fiscal years at once — the screen above shows FY2026-27 and
FY2027-28 side by side. You add the next one before it starts so there's always an open period
to post into. An Owner creates a new fiscal year from **Settings → Fiscal years**.
