## Not currently available

A **customer statement** — a single running document listing every invoice, credit note, and
payment for one customer, with a rolling balance — is not built into Ledgerly yet. If you're
looking for one to send a customer, there isn't a screen for it today.

## What to use instead

- **What one customer owes you, and how overdue it is:** the
  [AR Ageing report](/guide/reports/ar-ageing), which breaks every customer's balance into
  current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ day buckets. You can read a single customer's position
  off it at a glance.
- **The documents behind that balance:** the customer's open invoices and their
  [payments](/guide/sales/customer-payments), each of which links to its journal entry and, in
  turn, to the ledger.
- **A point-in-time list:** drill from the AR Ageing figure, or from the Trade Receivables
  line on the [Trial Balance](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/reading-the-core-reports), into the
  filtered Journals for that account.
