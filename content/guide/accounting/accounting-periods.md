## Where to find it

**Accounting → Periods.** It lists each fiscal year with its 12 monthly periods and the
actions available on each. The _why_ behind closing is in
[Bookkeeping basics](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/accounting-periods); this is the _how_.

![The Periods screen for an open fiscal year: 12 monthly periods, each marked Open, each with "Soft close" and "Hard close" buttons, and a "Close fiscal year" button](/guide/accounting/periods-open.jpg "An open fiscal year — every period offers Soft close and Hard close")

## Soft close

**Soft close** finishes a period for normal work. An Owner or Accountant can still post an
adjustment into it if something genuinely needs to land in that month.

- **To reopen** a soft-closed period: click **Reopen** and **type a reason**. The reason is
  recorded — a soft close you undo leaves a trail.

## Hard close

**Hard close** seals the period. Nothing posts into it again.

- Ledgerly makes you **type the period's name** to confirm, and states plainly that it
  **cannot be undone**.
- Only an **Owner** can hard-close.

![The Periods screen for a closed fiscal year: all 12 periods marked Closed, with a "Year closed" badge and no actions](/guide/accounting/periods-closed.jpg "A hard-closed fiscal year — every period Closed, the year sealed, no actions left")

## Year-end close

At the end of a fiscal year, once every period is settled, an **Owner** runs **Close fiscal
year**. This:

- posts a **year-end closing entry** that sweeps the year's income and expense balances to
  zero and rolls the net profit into **Retained Earnings**, and
- **seals the whole fiscal year** — every period Closed, marked _Year closed_.

Like a hard close, it's one-way. Run it only when the year is truly final — typically after
the accounts are prepared and any audit adjustments are in.

## The normal rhythm

Each month: reconcile the bank, check the month, **soft close**. **Hard close** older periods
once you're certain — often when a VAT return is filed and final. **Year-end close** once a
year, last.
