## What it answers

_"How did the owners' stake in the business change over this period, and why?"_ It takes each
equity account from its opening balance to its closing balance and shows the movement between.

![The Statement of Changes in Equity for Acme Trading: Component, Opening, Movement and Closing columns; "3100 — Retained Earnings" 0.00 → +16,000.00 → 16,000.00; "3200 — Current Year Earnings" 0.00 → +43,047.35 → 43,047.35; Total 0.00 → 59,047.35 → 59,047.35](/guide/reports/changes-in-equity.jpg "Changes in Equity — opening, movement, closing, per equity account")

## How to read it

- One row per **equity account** — share capital, retained earnings, current year earnings,
  and any others you've added.
- **Movement** = the period's activity: this year's [profit](/guide/reports/profit-loss) into
  Current Year Earnings, the prior year's profit rolled into Retained Earnings at year-end,
  new capital introduced, drawings taken out.
- **Closing** is what appears in the equity section of the
  [Balance Sheet](/guide/reports/balance-sheet).

## Worked example

**Current Year Earnings** moves by **43,047.35** — the exact [net
profit](/guide/reports/profit-loss) for the period. The **Total closing of 59,047.35** matches
the Balance Sheet's total equity.

## What it reconciles to

The equity rows of the [Balance Sheet](/guide/reports/balance-sheet) (closing column) and the
[Trial Balance](/guide/reports/trial-balance).

## Parameters

Period start and end.
