## What it answers

_"Do the books balance, and what's every account sitting at?"_ The Trial Balance lists **every
account** with its balance in the Debit or Credit column, as at a date you choose — any date,
including a past one.

![The Trial Balance for Acme Trading: a green "Balanced" banner, every account with a debit or credit balance, and a Total row of 149,000.00 on each side](/guide/reports/trial-balance.jpg "The Trial Balance — every account, one line, and equal debit and credit totals")

## How to read it

- **Assets and expenses** carry **debit** balances; **liabilities, equity and income** carry
  **credit** balances. (Why: [debits and
  credits](/guide/bookkeeping-basics/debits-and-credits).)
- The **Total** row must show the **same figure on both sides** — here, 149,000.00 — and the
  green **Balanced** banner confirms it. If the totals ever disagreed, the banner would turn
  red: something is wrong at the entry level and every other report is affected.

## Worked example

Acme's Trade Receivables shows **72,000.00** in the Debit column. Click it → the Journals list
filtered to that account → the invoices and receipts that net to 72,000.

## What it reconciles to

It _is_ the reconciliation — it's the ledger, laid out account by account. Every other
financial report is a rearrangement of these same balances: the
[Balance Sheet](/guide/reports/balance-sheet) is the asset/liability/equity rows, the
[P&L](/guide/reports/profit-loss) is the income/expense rows.

## Parameters

As-at date, and a pre/post-adjustment toggle for period-end adjusting entries.
