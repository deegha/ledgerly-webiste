## What it answers

_"What is the business worth, right now?"_ The Balance Sheet is a **snapshot as at one date**
(not a span), showing the three sides of the accounting equation.

![The Balance Sheet for Acme Trading: a green "Balanced — Assets = Liabilities + Equity" banner; Assets total 88,547.35; Liabilities total 29,500.00; Equity — Retained Earnings 16,000.00 and Current Year Earnings 43,047.35 — total 59,047.35](/guide/reports/balance-sheet.jpg "Balance Sheet — assets on one side, liabilities plus equity on the other, and the equation made visible")

## How to read it

- **Assets = Liabilities + Equity.** Here, 88,547.35 = 29,500.00 + 59,047.35.
- The green banner is the check — if the two sides didn't match, the books wouldn't balance.
- **Current Year Earnings (43,047.35)** is this year's [net
  profit](/guide/reports/profit-loss), carried into equity but not yet rolled into Retained
  Earnings (that happens at [year-end
  close](/guide/accounting/accounting-periods)).

## Worked example

**Inventory** shows **5,247.35**. That's the balance of GL account 1500 — and the
[Inventory Valuation report](/guide/inventory/inventory-valuation-report) totals to the same
5,247.35 from the other direction (stock movements). The banner on that report says so.

## Parameters

As-at date; a comparative column against a prior date.

## What it reconciles to

The asset, liability and equity rows of the [Trial Balance](/guide/reports/trial-balance) for
the same date.
