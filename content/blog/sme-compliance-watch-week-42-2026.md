**This week's article:** [Is your VAT tax invoice 2481/22-compliant? A checklist for Sri Lankan SMEs](/blog/is-your-vat-invoice-2481-22-compliant)

## What moved for your books

**1. 2481/22 VAT invoices have been mandatory since 1 Oct.** The revised tax invoice format (Gazette 2481/22, moved from 1 July to 1 October by Gazette 2500/106) is now in force. It requires a `YYMMM_QQQQ_XXXXX` serial number, both an invoice date and a date of supply, and TINs. Businesses still invoicing from Excel or Word templates are the most exposed, and their VAT-registered buyers may reject invoices that put their input credit at risk. Our checklist above covers every field.

**2. RAMIS e-invoicing is widening.** The IRD has published a Web API guide for sending invoices from business systems to RAMIS. The Treasury says phase 2, covering about 36,000 VAT-registered suppliers, is in user testing.

**3. SSCL and VAT deadlines on 20 and 31 Oct.** On 20 Oct, the September SSCL payment, the Jul–Sep SSCL return and the September VAT payment are due. Jul–Sep is the first quarter under the lower SSCL threshold of Rs. 9m a quarter (Rs. 36m a year), so many businesses are filing for the first time. VAT returns are due on 31 Oct.

**4. From 1 Nov, you need a TIN certificate.** You'll need one to open a bank account (each joint holder separately), register a business, get a credit card or transfer company shares. An IRD e-Services verification printout is accepted instead.

**5. The income-tax dates are stacking up:**

- **31 Oct:** last day to ask the IRD to revise your 2nd instalment if you expect lower income.
- **15 Nov:** 2nd instalment for Y/A 2026/27.
- **30 Nov:** Y/A 2025/26 returns. A company return without audited accounts is treated as not filed.
- **2 Dec:** pay the principal (and any unwaived penalty) on old arrears to get the interest written off.

**6. The 5% WHT now covers more professions.** Since 3 June, payers must withhold 5% on service fees over Rs. 100,000 a month paid to a much longer list of professions. The payer issues the certificates and can be fined up to Rs. 200,000 a year.

**7. Budget 2027 is on 12 Nov.** The Treasury says to expect reforms, not tax cuts. We'll cover what it means for your books.

_Also this week:_ Friday's island-wide power failure is a reminder that books kept on one shop PC can go down with it. Cloud books keep running when the shop PC doesn't.
